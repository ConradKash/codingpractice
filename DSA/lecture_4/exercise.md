# Understanding Notation Types in Mathematics and Programming

## Infix Notation
Infix notation is the common mathematical notation where operators are placed between operands.

**Examples:**
- `3 + 4`
- `(5 * 2) + (8 / 4)`
- `a + b * c`

## Prefix (Polish) Notation
Prefix notation places operators before their operands.

**Examples:**
- `+ 3 4` (equals 3 + 4)
- `+ * 5 2 / 8 4` (equals (5 * 2) + (8 / 4))
- `* + a b c` (equals (a + b) * c)

## Postfix (Reverse Polish) Notation
Postfix notation places operators after their operands.

**Examples:**
- `3 4 +` (equals 3 + 4)
- `5 2 * 8 4 / +` (equals (5 * 2) + (8 / 4))
- `a b + c *` (equals (a + b) * c)
